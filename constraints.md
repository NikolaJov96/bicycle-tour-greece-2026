# Trip Constraints

## Group
- 4 road bike riders
- Traveling by 2 cars from Belgrade, Serbia

## Dates
- Depart: June 13, 2026
- Return: June 28, 2026 (must be comfortably home — no 10+ hour driving days on return)
- Total duration: 16 days (including travel days)

## Logistics
- 2 or 3 bases where cars are parked and riders sleep
- Each day: ride from the base, or short drive to a starting point
- No islands (too remote, too much ferry time)
- Peloponnese is acceptable despite the distance

## Multi-day Ride
- One segment of 4–5 consecutive riding days
- Cars left at a base; sleep in accommodation each night (no camping)
- Ride point-to-point or loop between guesthouses/hotels

## Daily Riding Capacity
- Comfortable day: ~60 km, ~800 m elevation gain
- Can go longer if elevation is lower
- Can go for 50% longer and 50% more elevation but not on consecutive days

## Road Preference
- Third-level roads with low traffic only
- No highways, no busy arterial roads
- Road bikes (tarmac surface required, no gravel)

## Terrain Preference
- Hills and mountains preferred over flat
- A few sea-level coastal days included
- On coastal days: water sports activity (kayaking, sailing lesson) — should be in an area known for these

## Sights & Attractions
- Each day's route should ideally pass by a landmark, site, or natural attraction
- Priority criteria: scenic views (mountain + sea), road quality, historical/cultural sites along the route

## Special Activities
- One full day off bikes to hike Mount Olympus → one base must be within easy reach of Olympus

## What to Avoid
- Many long driving days between bases
- Flat, unscenic routes
- Busy roads with traffic
- Remote areas requiring many driving hours to reach from Belgrade
- Islands (ferry logistics, time cost)

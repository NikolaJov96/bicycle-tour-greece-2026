# Packing List

Two cars from Belgrade. June heat (30–38 °C at sea level, cooler in mountains). 16 days: road cycling, one Olympus hike, water sports.

---

## Documents & Admin

- Passport (valid 6+ months)
- International Driving Permit (IDP) — required alongside EU/Serbian licence in Greece
- Car insurance green card (valid in North Macedonia + Greece)
- Highway tags for Serbia and Macedonia
- Vehicle registration + vignettes for North Macedonia
- Travel insurance policy (with sports, mountain rescue, and medical evacuation coverage)
- Emergency contacts list (printed)
- Accommodation booking confirmations (printed or offline)

---

## Bike & Cycling Gear

### Clothing (per rider)
- Bib shorts ×3–4
- Cycling jerseys ×3–4
- Base layer ×2 (lightweight)
- Arm warmers ×1
- Cycling socks ×4–5 pairs
- Cycling gloves — short finger ×1, long finger ×1
- Cycling shoes + cleats (check cleat bolts before departure)
- Helmet
- Sunglasses (extra lens or photochromic)
- Rain jacket (lightweight, packable — mountain passes can surprise in June)

### On-bike equipment (per rider)
- Saddle bag or frame bag
- Two water bottles (700 ml minimum; three for remote sections like South Mani)
- Bike computer / GPS — loaded with all planned routes (Komoot/RideWithGPS)
- Lights — front and rear (for tunnels and early starts)
- Sunscreen (SPF 50+) in jersey pocket
- Energy gels / bars for the ride
- Small cash (Greek villages often cash only)
- Phone mount

### Tools & spares (shared, per car)
- Inner tubes ×6–8 (split between cars)
- Tyre levers ×4
- CO₂ inflators + cartridges, or a mini pump per rider
- Patch kit
- Chain lube (wet + dry)
- Multi-tool (Allen keys, Torx, chain breaker)
- Spare brake pads
- Cable ties + duct tape
- Nitrile gloves
- Portable track pump (one per car)

---

## Mount Olympus Hike (Day 14)

- Trail running or approach shoes with grip (not cycling shoes)
- Hiking poles (collapsible, fits in car)
- 30–35 L daypack
- Hydration bladder or 2× 1 L bottles
- Headlamp + spare batteries (early alpine start)
- Down or fleece layer (Mytikas summit ~10–15 °C even in June)
- Waterproof jacket (mandatory above treeline)
- Gloves (thin — summit scramble)
- Sun hat / buff
- High-calorie snacks + lunch
- Emergency bivvy / space blanket

---

## Water Sports Days (Days 5 and/or 11)

- Swimwear
- Towel (quick-dry)
- Dry bag for phone and valuables
- Sunscreen (waterproof)

---

## Clothing (off-bike, per rider)

- T-shirts ×3–4
- Lightweight trousers or shorts ×2
- Casual shoes or sandals
- Light sweater / hoodie (evenings in Ioannina and Litochoro can be cool)
- Underwear ×5
- Flip-flops (for showers and beach)

---

## Toiletries & Health

- Sunscreen SPF 50+ (large supply — Greek sun is intense)
- Lip balm with SPF
- Chamois cream
- After-sun / aloe vera gel
- Insect repellent
- Personal medications
- Electrolyte tablets / powder (critical for hot days)
- Ibuprofen + paracetamol
- Blister plasters (hiking day)
- Antiseptic wipes + small first aid kit (per car)

---

## Electronics

- Phone + charger
- Bike computer + charger / USB cable
- Power bank (10,000 mAh+)
- Earbuds (for driving legs)
- Camera / action cam + mounts, if desired
- Laptop or tablet (optional — for route adjustments)

---

## Car (per vehicle)

- Car first aid kit
- Reflective vest + warning triangle (mandatory in Greece)
- Bike rack or boot organizer
- Roof box or boot bags for luggage
- Tow rope
- Jerry can (optional — rural Peloponnese petrol stations can be sparse)
- Paper road map (North Macedonia + Greece) as GPS backup
- Wet wipes + hand sanitiser
- Rubbish bags

---

## Food & Nutrition (for the road)

- Energy bars, gels, chews for all riding days
- Electrolyte sachets
- Nuts and dried fruit (car snacks for drive days)
- Instant coffee / tea (for early starts at bases)
- Reusable water bottles for off-bike use

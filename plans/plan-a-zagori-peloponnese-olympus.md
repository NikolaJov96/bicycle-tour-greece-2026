# Plan A — Zagori → Peloponnese → Olympus

**Route:** Belgrade → Zagori (Ioannina) → Kalamata → Litochoro → Belgrade  
**Dates:** June 13–28, 2026  
**Bases:** 3 (Ioannina, Kalamata, Litochoro)  
**Riding days:** 10 + optional Day 15 · **Hike:** 1 · **Drive days:** 4

---

## Trip at a Glance

| Segment | Base | Days | Riding days |
|---------|------|------|-------------|
| Epirus / Zagori | Ioannina | Days 1–4 | 3 (Days 2–4) |
| Peloponnese | Kalamata | Days 5–10 | 5 (Days 6–10) |
| Pieria / Olympus | Litochoro | Days 11–15 | 2–3 (Days 12–13, opt. 15) |
| Return | — | Day 16 | — |

**Peloponnese loop:** 4-day clockwise loop from Kalamata. Cars parked at Kalamata. Nights in Sparta, Areopoli (×2), Kalamata.

**Kayak:** Flexible, non-structural. Most convenient on Day 5 (Kalamata, recommended) or Day 11 (Pieria Riviera).

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Base 1 — Ioannina (Epirus / Zagori)

**Day 1 — June 13 · Drive**
- Belgrade → Ioannina (~10h via Niš, Skopje, Kozani, Egnatia Odos)
- Night: Ioannina

**Day 2 — June 14 · Zagori villages + Vikos Gorge viewpoints**
- Route: Ioannina → [Monodendri](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Monodendri,+Zagori,+Greece) → [Vitsa](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Vitsa,+Zagori,+Greece) → [Papingo](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Papingo,+Zagori,+Greece) → [Beloi viewpoint](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Beloi+viewpoint,+Vikos,+Greece) → Ioannina
- ~65 km · ~900 m
- Highlights: stone-arched Ottoman bridges, Vikos Gorge overlook (one of the deepest gorges in the world), Zagorochoria stone villages
- Routes: [Komoot — Greece Day 4 Vikos Gorge (59.5 km)](https://www.komoot.com/tour/703010289) · [Komoot guide — top road rides around Ioannina](https://www.komoot.com/guide/34009/road-cycling-routes-around-ioannina) · [Wikiloc — road bike trails in Ioannina](https://www.wikiloc.com/trails/cycling/greece/epirus/ioannina)

**Day 3 — June 15 · Katara Pass / Metsovo**
- Route: Ioannina → [Metsovo](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Metsovo,+Greece) via [Katara Pass](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Katara+Pass,+Greece) (1,705m) → back
- ~75 km · ~1,100 m
- Highlights: highest paved pass in Greece; Metsovo traditional Epirote village at the summit; connects Epirus to Thessaly
- Routes: [Komoot guide — top road rides around Ioannina](https://www.komoot.com/guide/34009/road-cycling-routes-around-ioannina) · [Outdooractive — cycling in Zagori/Epirus](https://www.outdooractive.com/en/cycle-routes/zagori/cycling-in-zagori/290992525/) · no specific verified Ioannina–Katara out-and-back tour found; search "komoot Ioannina Metsovo road ride" or "ridewithgps Katara Pass Greece"

**Day 4 — June 16 · Tsepelovo + Morfa Pass loop**
- Route: Ioannina → [Tsepelovo](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Tsepelovo,+Zagori,+Greece) → [Morfa Pass](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Morfa+Pass,+Zagori,+Greece) → Ioannina
- ~60 km · ~800 m
- Highlights: remote southern Zagori villages, mountain pass with Pindus views; easier finish day before long drive
- Routes: [Komoot guide — top road rides around Ioannina](https://www.komoot.com/guide/34009/road-cycling-routes-around-ioannina) · [Outdooractive — cycling in Zagori](https://www.outdooractive.com/en/cycle-routes/zagori/cycling-in-zagori/290992525/) · no specific verified tour found; search "komoot Tsepelovo road cycling" or "ridewithgps Morfa Pass"

### Base 2 — Kalamata (Peloponnese)

**Day 6 — June 18 · Ancient Messene loop**
- Route: Kalamata → [Ancient Messene](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Ancient+Messini,+Greece) (Mavromati village) → back
- ~55 km · ~800 m
- Highlights: one of Greece's best-preserved ancient cities — full circuit walls, theatre, stadium, sanctuaries; short but rewarding climb to the site at ~370m
- Routes: [Komoot — Ancient Messene waypoint (Clepsydra Spring)](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/637740) · [Komoot — Ancient Messene site waypoint](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/637389) · [Komoot collection — road cycling Peloponnese](https://www.komoot.com/collection/1378031/road-cycling-in-greece-athens-and-the-peloponnese) (contains a Kalamata→Messini route, ~70 km, ~1,300 m)

**Day 7 — June 19 · Loop Day 1 — Langada Pass → Sparta → Mystras** ⚠️ hardest day
- Route: Kalamata → [Langada Pass](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Langada+Pass,+Laconia,+Greece) (1,524m) → [Sparta](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Sparta,+Greece) → [Mystras](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Mystras,+Greece) → Sparta
- ~70 km · ~1,400 m
- Highlights: Langada Pass — the most famous road cycling climb in the Peloponnese (west face from Kalamata, 28km); Mystras UNESCO Byzantine ghost city, seat of the last Byzantine emperors; 6km/300m ascent above Sparta
- Note: Day 6 (800m) is at comfortable level, making Day 7's hard effort acceptable per consecutive-day constraints. Keep Day 6 at a steady pace.
- Routes: [Komoot — Kalamata to Sparta (68.8 km, west face Langada)](https://www.komoot.com/tour/691380605) · [Komoot — Sparta to Kalamata (east face, reverse direction reference)](https://www.komoot.com/tour/1616833002)
- Night: Sparta

**Day 8 — June 20 · Loop Day 2 — Sparta → Gytheio → Areopoli**
- Route: Sparta → [Gytheio](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Gytheio,+Greece) (via [Eurotas valley](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Eurotas+River,+Laconia,+Greece)) → [Areopoli](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Areopoli,+Greece)
- ~75 km · ~800 m
- Highlights: Eurotas river valley through ancient olive groves; Gytheio — ancient Spartan port with colorful harbor; entry into the Mani peninsula
- Routes: [Komoot — Areopoli to Gytheio (28.4 km, same road, reverse direction reference)](https://www.komoot.com/tour/300021917) · [Bikemap — routes around Gytheion](https://www.bikemap.net/en/l/251297/) · no single Sparta–Gytheio–Areopoli tour found; search "komoot Sparta Gytheio Areopoli"
- Night: Areopoli

**Day 9 — June 21 · Loop Day 3 — South Mani loop**
- Route: Areopoli → [Diros Caves](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Diros+Caves,+Pyrgos+Dirou,+Greece) → [Gerolimenas](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Gerolimenas,+Mani,+Greece) → [Cape Tainaron](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Cape+Tainaron,+Greece) → [Porto Kayio](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Porto+Kayio,+Mani,+Greece) → [Vathia](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Vathia,+Mani,+Greece) → Areopoli
- ~85 km · ~1,100 m
- Highlights: Diros Caves (stalactite caves toured by boat — unique in Greece); Cape Tainaron (southernmost point of mainland Greece; mythological entrance to the Underworld); Porto Kayio (bay with ancient Poseidon temple); Vathia (most dramatic Mani tower-house village, photographed from every angle)
- Routes: [GrCycling — routes in Areopoli](https://booking.grcycling.com/areopoli,-lakonia/routes) · [GrCycling — routes in Gerolimenas](https://booking.grcycling.com/gerolimenas) · [Bikemap — Mani route 37 km](https://www.bikemap.net/en/r/1131874/) · no single verified Cape Tainaron loop from Areopoli found; search "komoot Cape Tainaron road cycling" or "ridewithgps Mani south loop"
- Night: Areopoli

**Day 10 — June 22 · Loop Day 4 — West Mani coast → Kalamata**
- Route: Areopoli → [Stoupa](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Stoupa,+Mani,+Greece) → [Kardamyli](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Kardamyli,+Greece) → Kalamata
- ~70 km · ~800 m
- Highlights: Stoupa — crystal clear bay, water sports base; Kardamyli — beautiful old village in a fig and olive grove, Patrick Leigh Fermor's home for decades; praised coastal road with sea cliffs and minimal traffic
- Routes: [Bikemap — Mani coastal route 37 km](https://www.bikemap.net/en/r/1131874/) · [Wikiloc — road bike trails in Kardamyli](https://www.wikiloc.com/trails/cycling/greece/peloponnese/kardamyli) · no single Areopoli–Kalamata via coast tour found; search "komoot Kardamyli Kalamata road ride"
- Night: Kalamata

### Base 3 — Litochoro (Pieria / Olympus)

**Day 12 — June 24 · Olympus road — Litochoro → Prionia**
- Route: Litochoro → [Prionia trailhead](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Prionia,+Mount+Olympus,+Greece) (1,100m) → explore upper Olympus roads → back
- ~60 km · ~1,000 m
- Highlights: the classic 30-hairpin Olympus cycling road; excellent tarmac with center stripe; views of the Aegean coast and Olympus peaks; road ends at the hiking trailhead
- Routes: [Bikemap — Prionia climb (18 km, 1,105 m)](https://www.bikemap.net/en/r/4429650/) · [Bikemap — Litochoro to Prionia](https://www.bikemap.net/en/r/4144680/) · [CyclingHellas — 1-day guided tour to Prionia](https://cyclinghellas.gr/en/tours/422-1-day-cycling-tour-to-mythical-mt-olympus-prionia)

**Day 13 — June 25 · Platamon Castle → old Pieria villages → coastal road**
- Route: Litochoro → [Platamon Castle](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Platamon+Castle,+Greece) → [Panteleimon](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Panteleimon,+Pieria,+Greece) → [Old Pori](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Palaios+Pori,+Pieria,+Greece) → [Ano Skotina](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Ano+Skotina,+Pieria,+Greece) → coastal road back
- ~65 km · ~700 m
- Highlights: Platamon Castle (Byzantine-Ottoman fortress above the Aegean); traditional villages in the Pieria hills; coastal road with sea views; lighter day before the hike
- Routes: [Komoot — Litochoro to Leptokaria (18.7 km, coastal)](https://www.komoot.com/tour/104707120) · [Bikemap — Panteleimon–Leptokaria–Litochoro (33 km)](https://www.bikemap.net/en/r/4103798/) · [Bikemap — routes around Litochoro](https://www.bikemap.net/en/l/735399/)

**Day 14 — June 26 · Mount Olympus hike**
- Drive to Prionia (or ride up — 18km, 950m — if saving legs for hike start); hike Stefani → Mytikas → Skala circuit from Prionia
- 10.39 km · 2,386 m elevation gain · min 1,125 m (Prionia) · max 2,958 m (Mytikas)
- Wikiloc: https://loc.wiki/t/113126867?h=gp288q5n2p&wa=so&la=en
- Highlights: highest peak in Greece (2,918m); Stefani–Mytikas–Skala traverse; Spilios Agapitos refuge at 2,100m; Mytikas requires scrambling (hands needed)
- Note: book refuge accommodation in advance if planning an early start

**Day 15 — June 27 · Pelion peninsula (day trip from Litochoro)**
- Drive Litochoro → Pelion start point (~2h); ride; drive back to Litochoro in the evening (~2h)
- ~65 km · ~900 m
- Highlights: chestnut and apple orchards; traditional stone arhontika mansions; [Portaria](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Portaria,+Pelion,+Greece) and [Makrinitsa](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Makrinitsa,+Pelion,+Greece) balcony villages above Volos; [Tsagarada](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Tsagarada,+Pelion,+Greece) with its ancient plane tree; switchback roads down to Aegean beaches ([Mylopotamos](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Mylopotamos+beach,+Pelion,+Greece), [Damouchari](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Damouchari,+Pelion,+Greece)); quiet tarmac, minimal traffic
- Fresh legs after the hike day; drive buffer in morning before riding
- Routes: [Komoot guide — road cycling in Thessaly/Central Greece](https://www.komoot.com/guide/63585/road-cycling-routes-in-thessaly-central-greece) · no specific Pelion loop tour verified; search "komoot Pelion road cycling loop", "ridewithgps Pilio road bike", or "wikiloc cycling Volos Pelion"
- Night: Litochoro

# Plan C — Evia + Attica Loop via Marmari→Rafina Ferry

**Route:** Belgrade → Litochoro → Halkida → Evia (self-supported loop) → ferry Marmari→Rafina → Attica → Halkida → Litochoro → Ioannina → Belgrade  
**Dates:** June 13–28, 2026  
**Riding days:** 10 · **Hike:** 1 · **Drive days:** 5

---

## Trip at a Glance

| Segment | Overnight towns | Days | Riding days |
|---------|----------------|------|-------------|
| Olympus | Litochoro campsite | Days 1–2 | 1 (Day 2) |
| Evia + Attica loop | Halkida · Kymi · Styra · Karystos · Rafina · Schinias | Days 3–9 | 6 (Days 4–9) |
| Olympus — second stay | Litochoro campsite | Days 10–11 | 0 (hike day) |
| Zagori / Pindos | Ioannina | Days 12–15 | 3 (Days 13–15) |
| Return | — | Day 16 | — |

**Loop format:** Both cars park at Halkida on Day 3 and remain there until Day 10. Riders carry all gear (panniers or bikepacker bags) for 6 days. Clockwise Evia loop: north over Dirfys to the Aegean east coast, south to Karystos and Marmari. Ferry Marmari→Rafina (bikes as passenger luggage — no car ferry needed). Attica traverse: south along the Aegean coast to Vravrona, inland to Markopoulo wine country and Marathon, then northwest to Oropos and along the Euboean Gulf back to Halkida.

**Accommodation:**
- Litochoro (Days 1–2): campsite — e.g., [Camping Olympos Zeus](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Camping+Olympos+Zeus,+Litochoro,+Greece)
- Halkida (Days 3 and 9): hotel near the bridge — cars parked here both nights
- Evia nights: hotels / guesthouses in each town (pre-book for June)
- Rafina / Marathon (Days 7–8): hotel / guesthouse
- Litochoro (Days 10–11): campsite (second stay)
- Ioannina (Days 12–15): hotel in city centre

**Water sports:** Sailing on the Euboean Gulf from Halkida, afternoon of Day 3 — [Aegean Outdoors](https://aegeanoutdoors.com/tour/evia-sailing-adventure/).

## Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Litochoro campsite

**Day 1 — June 13 · Drive**
- Belgrade → Litochoro (~8h via Niš, Skopje, Thessaloniki)
- Night: Litochoro campsite

**Day 2 — June 14 · Olympus road — Litochoro → Prionia**
- Route: Litochoro → [Prionia trailhead](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Prionia,+Mount+Olympus,+Greece) (1,100 m) → back
- ~60 km · ~1,000 m
- Highlights: the classic 30-hairpin Olympus road; excellent tarmac with centre stripe; Aegean and Olympus peak views; road ends at the hiking trailhead (preview of Day 11)
- Routes: [Bikemap — Prionia climb (18 km, 1,105 m)](https://www.bikemap.net/en/r/4429650/) · [CyclingHellas — 1-day guided tour to Prionia](https://cyclinghellas.gr/en/tours/422-1-day-cycling-tour-to-mythical-mt-olympus-prionia)
- Night: Litochoro campsite

### Evia + Attica loop — self-supported · cars at Halkida

**Day 3 — June 15 · Drive + water sports**
- Litochoro → Halkida (~3h via Larissa, Lamia)
- Arrive midday — **water sports**: sailing on the Euboean Gulf from Halkida harbour ([Aegean Outdoors — Evia Sailing Adventure](https://aegeanoutdoors.com/tour/evia-sailing-adventure/))
- Brief walk to the [Euripus strait](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Euripus+Strait,+Halkida,+Greece): the tidal channel between Evia and the mainland reverses direction ~14 times/day — visibly churning water
- Cars park at Halkida; riders load panniers for the 6-day loop
- Night: Halkida

**Stage 1 — Day 4 — June 16 · Halkida → Kymi via Steni Dirfys** ⚠️ hardest day
- Route: Halkida → [Steni Dirfys](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Steni+Dirfys,+Evia,+Greece) (450 m) → continue climbing to Dirfys pass (~1,100 m) → east-face descent → [Kymi](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Kymi,+Evia,+Greece)
- ~85 km · ~1,400 m
- Highlights: Steni Dirfys is a village at the foot of the north face of Dirfys (1,743 m — Evia's highest peak); the road climbs through dense fir forest to the saddle and then drops steeply to the Aegean coast; Kymi is the only town on Evia's east coast, perched on a cliff above the sea; descent to [Paralia Kymis](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Paralia+Kymis,+Evia,+Greece) for a swim
- ⚠️ The mountain crossing from Steni to the east face should be verified on Komoot before riding — paved but variable quality; search "komoot Steni Dirfys Kymi crossing"
- Night: Kymi

**Stage 2 — Day 5 — June 17 · Kymi → Styra (east coast south)**
- Route: Kymi → south along the east-coast cliff road → [Kolethra Spring](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Kolethra+Spring,+Evia,+Greece) → [Avlonari](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Avlonari,+Evia,+Greece) (Byzantine tower-house) → [Styra](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Styra,+Evia,+Greece) coastal area
- ~65 km · ~700 m
- Highlights: the east coast road south of Kymi hugs dramatic cliffs above the Aegean; Kolethra Spring — fresh water source with a shaded rest spot; Avlonari is an inland village with a distinctive medieval tower-house visible from far; Styra is the gateway to the Dragon Houses area
- Routes: search "komoot Kymi Styra east coast road cycling"
- Night: Styra / [Almyropotamos](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Almyropotamos,+Evia,+Greece) area

**Stage 3 — Day 6 — June 18 · Styra → Dragon Houses → Karystos**
- Route: Styra → [Dragon Houses of Styra (Drakospita)](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Drakospita,+Styra,+Evia,+Greece) → [Dragon Houses of Kapsala](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Dragon+Houses+Kapsala,+Evia,+Greece) → [Agios Dimitrios Gorge](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Agios+Dimitrios+Gorge,+Karystos,+Evia,+Greece) → [Karystos](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Karystos,+Evia,+Greece)
- ~55 km · ~1,000 m
- Highlights: Dragon Houses (Drakospita) — enormous granite slabs assembled without mortar into roofed structures found nowhere else in the world, probably 8th–7th century BCE, two distinct sites; [Mt. Ohi](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Mt+Ohi,+Evia,+Greece) (1,395 m) backdrop; Agios Dimitrios Gorge on the approach to Karystos — a narrow ravine with clear water; Karystos harbour for the night
- Note: shorter distance but high elevation load from the Dragon Houses climbs; plan extra time for on-foot exploration at both sites
- Routes: [GrCycling — routes around Karystos](https://booking.grcycling.com/karystos,-evia/routes) · search "komoot Styra Dragon Houses Karystos"
- Night: Karystos

**Stage 4 + ferry — Day 7 — June 19 · Karystos → Marmari · ferry → Rafina · evening: Marathon**
- Riding route: Karystos → inland via [Bouros](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Bouros,+Karystos,+Evia,+Greece) → Mt Ohi foothills road (~800 m viewpoint) → descent → [Marmari](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Marmari+Port,+Evia,+Greece)
- ~45 km · ~600 m
- Highlights: the Mt Ohi foothills road offers panoramic views over the southern Aegean and the island's tip; Marmari harbour — a small calm bay with the ferry terminal; arrive Marmari by midday for the afternoon sailing
- Routes: [GrCycling — routes around Karystos](https://booking.grcycling.com/karystos,-evia/routes) · search "komoot Karystos Marmari mountain route"
- Ferry: [Goutos Lines — Marmari→Rafina](https://www.goutos.gr/) · ~1.5h crossing · bikes board as passenger luggage, no car booking needed · multiple daily sailings, confirm current schedule at goutos.gr · book passenger tickets in advance for June
- Evening: ride from [Rafina](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Rafina,+Attica,+Greece) harbour (~10 km north) to the [Marathon Soros tumulus](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Tumulus+of+Marathon,+Marathon,+Attica,+Greece) — the burial mound of the 192 Athenian dead from the 490 BCE battle against the Persian army; still visible as a large grassed mound on the plain; adjacent [Archaeological Museum of Marathon](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Archaeological+Museum+of+Marathon,+Marathon,+Attica,+Greece)
- Night: [Marathon](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Marathon,+Attica,+Greece) / Rafina area

**Stage 5 — Day 8 — June 20 · Rafina → Vravrona → Markopoulo → Schinias**
- Route: Rafina → south along the Aegean coast → [Artemida (Loutsa)](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Artemida,+Loutsa,+Attica,+Greece) → [Vravrona (Brauron)](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Vravrona,+Brauron,+Attica,+Greece) → cut inland west → [Markopoulo](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Markopoulo,+Attica,+Greece) → north back to [Schinias](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Schinias+Beach,+Marathon,+Attica,+Greece)
- ~70 km · ~500 m
- Highlights: Vravrona/Brauron — one of the most evocative sanctuaries in Attica, dedicated to Artemis; the site sits on a coastal marsh delta and includes temple foundations, a stoa, and the [Archaeological Museum of Brauron](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Archaeological+Museum+of+Brauron,+Vravrona,+Attica,+Greece) — exceptional finds including terracotta bears and votive korai from the arkteia ritual (young Athenian girls serving as "bears" of Artemis); Markopoulo — the heart of the Mesogeia wine region, Savatiano grape, with several roadside wineries; Schinias — a long pine-fringed beach on the Marathon plain, national park; afternoon swim
- Routes: search "komoot Rafina Vravrona Markopoulo cycling" · [Bikemap — cycling routes in Attica](https://www.bikemap.net/en/l/2552/)
- Night: Schinias / [Nea Makri](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Nea+Makri,+Attica,+Greece)

**Stage 6 — Day 9 — June 21 · Schinias → Kalamos → Amphiaraeion → Oropos → Halkida** ⚠️ longest day · return to cars
- Route: Schinias → [Grammatiko](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Grammatiko,+Attica,+Greece) → [Kalamos](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Kalamos,+Attica,+Greece) → [Amphiaraeion of Oropos](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Amphiaraeion+of+Oropos,+Oropos,+Attica,+Greece) → [Oropos](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Oropos,+Attica,+Greece) → coastal road along the Euboean Gulf → [Aulis](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Aulis,+Boeotia,+Greece) → Halkida
- ~95 km · ~600 m
- Highlights: Grammatiko — a quiet inland village on the wooded road north; Kalamos — a traditional hillside village with views over the north Attica coast; Amphiaraeion — the oracle and healing sanctuary of Amphiaraos, almost never crowded; it stood just outside official Attic territory and attracted pilgrims from across Greece; remarkably preserved with a colonnaded stoa, small theatre, altar, and sacred spring where patients slept to receive healing dreams; [official site](http://odysseus.culture.gr/h/3/eh351.jsp?obj_id=2428); Oropos harbour — a small town on the Euboean Gulf strait, Evia clearly visible across the water; the coastal road from Oropos to Halkida runs along the same strait riders saw from the Evia west side in Plan B — same water, opposite shore; arrival at Halkida closes the loop and reunites the group with the cars
- Routes: search "komoot Marathon Oropos Halkida road cycling" · search "ridewithgps Attica north coast cycling"
- Night: Halkida *(end of loop — cars reunited)*

---

### Litochoro campsite — second stay

**Day 10 — June 22 · Drive back to Olympus**
- Halkida → Litochoro (~3h via Lamia, Larissa)
- Arrive midday — rest, short walk, swim at Litochoro beach if desired
- Night: Litochoro campsite

**Day 11 — June 23 · Mount Olympus hike**
- Drive to Prionia (or ride up — 18 km, 950 m); hike Stefani → Mytikas → Skala circuit from Prionia
- 10.39 km · 2,386 m elevation gain · min 1,125 m (Prionia) · max 2,958 m (Mytikas)
- Wikiloc: https://loc.wiki/t/113126867?h=gp288q5n2p&wa=so&la=en
- Highlights: highest peak in Greece (2,918 m); Stefani–Mytikas–Skala traverse; Spilios Agapitos refuge at 2,100 m; Mytikas requires scrambling (hands needed)
- Note: book Spilios Agapitos refuge in advance if planning an early start
- Night: Litochoro campsite

---

### Ioannina — Zagori / Pindos

**Day 12 — June 24 · Drive to Zagori**
- Litochoro → Ioannina (~3.5h via Katerini, Kozani, Grevena)
- Arrive midday — rest or walk in Ioannina old town; Ottoman-era castle island on the lake
- Night: Ioannina

**Day 13 — June 25 · Zagori villages + Vikos Gorge**
- Route: Ioannina → [Monodendri](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Monodendri,+Zagori,+Greece) → [Vitsa](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Vitsa,+Zagori,+Greece) → [Papingo](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Papingo,+Zagori,+Greece) → [Beloi viewpoint](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Beloi+viewpoint,+Vikos,+Greece) → Ioannina
- ~65 km · ~900 m
- Highlights: stone-arched Ottoman bridges; Vikos Gorge overlook (one of the deepest gorges in the world); Zagorochoria stone villages largely unspoiled by mass tourism
- Routes: [Komoot — Greece Day 4 Vikos Gorge (59.5 km)](https://www.komoot.com/tour/703010289) · [Komoot guide — road rides around Ioannina](https://www.komoot.com/guide/34009/road-cycling-routes-around-ioannina)
- Night: Ioannina

**Day 14 — June 26 · Katara Pass / Metsovo**
- Route: Ioannina → [Metsovo](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Metsovo,+Greece) via [Katara Pass](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Katara+Pass,+Greece) (1,705 m) → back
- ~75 km · ~1,100 m
- Highlights: highest paved pass in Greece; Metsovo traditional Epirote village at the summit; excellent views over the Pindus range
- Routes: [Komoot guide — road rides around Ioannina](https://www.komoot.com/guide/34009/road-cycling-routes-around-ioannina)
- Night: Ioannina

**Day 15 — June 27 · Tsepelovo + Morfa Pass loop**
- Route: Ioannina → [Tsepelovo](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Tsepelovo,+Zagori,+Greece) → [Morfa Pass](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=Morfa+Pass,+Zagori,+Greece) → Ioannina
- ~60 km · ~800 m
- Highlights: remote southern Zagori villages; Morfa Pass with Pindus views; lighter finish day before the long return drive
- Night: Ioannina
